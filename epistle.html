My Dearest Adejoke,

On Your Special Day

Where do I even begin to capture who you are? Words feel small when describing a heart as big as yours.

I remember all the times you put everyone else before yourself. When we needed someone to lean on, you were there. When we needed encouragement, you spoke life into us. When we fell, you helped us rise again. That's just who you are — a giver, a nurturer, a guardian angel disguised as a sister.

You have this rare gift of making people feel seen and loved. You don't just hear — you listen. You don't just help — you sacrifice. You don't just care — you pour your whole heart out until there's nothing left, and somehow still find more to give.

Living miles away in the UK, you never let distance change your love. You check in, you pray for us, you worry about us, and you celebrate every small win as if it were your own. That is the definition of a mother's heart — and you have it in abundance.

On this birthday, I want you to know that your sacrifices have not gone unnoticed. Your generosity has planted seeds of kindness that will grow forever. Your love has shaped who I am today.

You deserve the world, Adejoke. You deserve all the happiness, all the laughter, and all the blessings that life can offer. And I pray that this new year of your life brings you double the joy you've given to others.

Thank you for being my sister. Thank you for being like a mother to me. Thank you for being simply... you.

Forever grateful,
WAREEZ
